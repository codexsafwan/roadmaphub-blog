Roadmap Ambassadors are elite practitioners who share their hard-won industry experience through hands-on code reviews, webinars, and personalized roadmaps.

### Our Ambassador Values

1. **Practical, Production-Tested Advice**: No textbook theory—only real architectural decisions from systems running millions of requests per second.
2. **Accessible & Community First**: Democratizing high-level mentorship for engineers regardless of background or geographic location.

Our ambassadors dedicate hours every month to host open Q&A sessions, review community pull requests, and coach career changers through difficult leveling barriers.

We are proud to celebrate these leaders and support their mission to build a more equitable tech community.
