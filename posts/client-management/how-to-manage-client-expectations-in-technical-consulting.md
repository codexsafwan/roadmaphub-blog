Technical proficiency gets you hired, but clear communication and expectation management make clients return for years. Here are battle-tested strategies from experienced consultants.

### The Client Success Framework

1. **Define Success Metrics Early**: Establish clear, quantifiable milestones before writing the first line of code or starting a syllabus.
2. **Proactive Async Updates**: Send 2-minute weekly summaries highlighting blockers, achievements, and upcoming milestones.

Misaligned expectations are the number one cause of client churn. By standardizing intake forms and scheduling weekly check-ins, you convert anxious clients into collaborative partners.

Trust is built through predictable reliability and radical transparency.
