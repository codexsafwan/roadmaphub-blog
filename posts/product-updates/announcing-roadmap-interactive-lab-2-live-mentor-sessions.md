We are thrilled to launch Interactive Lab 2.0 on Roadmap! Engineering learners can now book direct 1-on-1 mentorship sessions with verified Staff and Principal Engineers through real-time WhatsApp booking.

### What's New in 2.0

1. **Direct WhatsApp Booking Pre-Fill**: Select a topic (System Design, L6 Promotion, Mock Interview) and launch directly into WhatsApp with pre-filled context.
2. **Detailed Student Guides**: Each mentor card now features a 3-step structured student guide and actionable leveling rubrics.
3. **Verified Company Badges**: Direct verification badges for mentors from Google, Netflix, Stripe, Meta, and OpenAI.

Traditional mentorship platforms charge high subscription fees while locking communication inside clunky custom apps. With Roadmap's WhatsApp integration, engineers communicate directly where they already chat daily.

Over 350 successful sessions have already taken place during our closed beta, with an average student rating of 4.98 stars.

Explore the new Mentor Hub today and take your software engineering career to the next level.
